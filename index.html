HOW TO PLAY

MAZE MISSION

Choose between multiple routes. Complete all keys, orbs and switches before the exit opens. Some Precision Rounds give almost exactly enough moves.

WEB PUZZLE

Watch the dots pulse in order for 10 seconds. Movement is locked during memorization. Then reconnect the same dot sequence from memory.

PHONE DRAG

Tap adjacent tiles or hold your finger down and slide across tiles continuously to draw your web path.

MISTAKES

A wrong puzzle dot costs half a heart. Running out of moves or failing the round costs one full heart.

BOSS ROUNDS

Every 10th level combines more mechanics and gives better rewards.
